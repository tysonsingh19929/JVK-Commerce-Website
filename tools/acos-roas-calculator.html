The Complete Guide to ACoS vs ROAS on Amazon & Marketplaces

Marketplace advertising requires balancing top-line velocity with bottom-line net return. Sellers frequently ask which metric matters most:

ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales)

Calculated as (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Sales) × 100. Used inside Amazon Seller Central. A lower number indicates higher ad margin efficiency.

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

Calculated as Ad Sales ÷ Ad Spend. Used in Walmart Connect, Google Ads, and Meta. A higher number indicates greater gross return per dollar invested.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a good target ACoS for Amazon in 2026?

For mature catalog products, a target ACoS between 18% and 25% is standard. For brand-new product launches where keyword ranking is the primary objective, brands often tolerate an ACoS equal to their break-even margin (35% to 45%) for the first 60 days to capture organic BSR momentum.

What is TACoS and why does JVK Commerce track it?

TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) measures Ad Spend divided by Total Revenue (ad sales + organic sales). As JVK Commerce boosts your organic keyword ranking, your TACoS drops, meaning you spend fewer advertising dollars to generate each dollar of total store revenue.
