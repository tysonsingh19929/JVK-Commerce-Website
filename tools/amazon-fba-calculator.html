How Amazon FBA Fees Work in 2026: The Essential Formula

Selling on Amazon through Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) frees brands from packing, shipping, and customer support, but Amazon charges two primary operational deductions for every transaction:

  • Amazon Referral Fee: Amazon charges a percentage of the total sales price (including shipping charged to customer) as a commission for marketplace access. For the majority of consumer categories, this sits at exactly 15%, while electronics is 8%, and apparel is 17%.
  • FBA Pick & Pack Fulfillment Fee: This is a flat rate charged per unit based on product weight and volumetric size tier. Even an extra 0.2 inches in packaging can bump an item from Small Standard to Large Standard, costing an additional $0.93 per order.
  • Inbound Placement & Aged Inventory Surcharges: In 2026, Amazon penalizes low-inventory and slow-moving stock. Managing precise 45-day replenishment cycles is critical to preventing margin erosion.
