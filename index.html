Clear guidance on our marketplace management scope, operational procedures, PPC advertising architectures, and in-house operator training.

JVK Commerce provides dedicated marketplace management across two core international spheres:

  • North America, UK, Europe & Australia: Amazon (US, CA, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, AU), Walmart Marketplace, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Instagram Shop, and headless Shopify.
  • Middle East & Gulf (GCC): United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia across Amazon.ae, Amazon.sa, Noon (FBN & Direct Ship), Trendyol, Namshi, 6thStreet, Mumzworld, FirstCry, and Babyshop.

We manage single-region launches as well as unified cross-border multi-channel accounts.

Yes. We have senior operational capabilities across both 3P (Seller Central) and 1P (Vendor Central) ecosystems:

  • 3P Seller Central: Full ownership of retail pricing, inventory replenishment, FBA shipping plans, Buy Box defense, and customer interaction.
  • 1P Vendor Central: Processing wholesale purchase orders (POs), operational chargeback defense, co-op agreements, shortage reconciliations, and direct Vendor Manager communication.
  • Hybrid Models: Many of our clients operate a hybrid 1P/3P strategy to protect margin and prevent retail stockouts.

Our advertising methodology focuses on net profitability rather than vanity revenue:

  • Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture: Clear segmentation between Sponsored Products (Exact, Phrase, Broad, ASIN defense), Sponsored Brands video, Sponsored Display, and Amazon DSP programmatic retargeting.
  • Negative Harvesting: Daily automated negative keyword harvesting eliminates ad spend waste on non-converting search terms.
  • Dayparting & Placements: Bid adjustments dynamically allocate budget during peak buying hours and high-converting top-of-search placements.
  • Blended TACoS Governance: Every dollar spent is monitored against total catalog revenue to lift organic rankings while shrinking advertising costs.

Stockouts destroy organic keyword rankings and BSR. We deploy 30/60/90-day predictive run-rate models that factor in seasonal spikes (Q4 holidays, Prime Day, Ramadan, White Friday), transit times, and customs clearance.

We generate FBA and Noon FBN shipment plans, supervise pallet labeling and appointment delivery, and configure FBM or Seller Flex fulfillment as an instant safety valve if warehouse limits are reached.

We establish Brand Registry IP alignment across every target marketplace jurisdiction. We design custom multi-page Brand Stores and author rich A+ Content (EBC) with comparison charts to elevate consumer trust.

Our automated monitoring detects unauthorized sellers, price undercutting, and counterfeit listing piggybackers 24/7, enabling immediate trademark infringement notices and Project Zero / Transparency enforcement.
