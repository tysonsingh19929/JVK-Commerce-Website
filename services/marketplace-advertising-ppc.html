Frequently Asked Questions

Advertising & PPC FAQs

What is the difference between ACoS and TACoS, and which metric matters more? +

ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales = Ad Spend / Ad Sales) measures direct campaign return. TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales = Ad Spend / Total Overall Revenue) measures the holistic financial health of your brand. We focus primarily on TACoS because paid ad velocity drives organic keyword rank. A decreasing TACoS over time proves your organic sales are expanding alongside paid volume.

How do you eliminate wasted ad spend in marketplace campaigns? +

We run algorithmic negative keyword harvesting daily, identifying irrelevant search terms, non-converting competitor brands, and click-drain phrases. We isolate high-converting exact match keywords into single-keyword ad groups (SKAGs) with dedicated budgets to ensure ad dollars are never squandered on loose broad queries.

When should an enterprise brand invest in Amazon DSP programmatic advertising? +

Amazon DSP is best suited for established brands seeking full-funnel dominance. Unlike standard search PPC, DSP buys programmatic display and video inventory across Amazon properties and external third-party web apps, enabling hyper-targeted retargeting of past purchasers, abandoned carts, and shoppers who browsed competitor ASINs.
