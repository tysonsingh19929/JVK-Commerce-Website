Frequently Asked Questions

Amazon Management FAQs

How does JVK Commerce handle Seller Central vs Vendor Central? +

Under Seller Central (3P), your brand is the merchant of record, setting retail prices, controlling catalog presentation, and taking direct profit margins. Under Vendor Central (1P), Amazon purchases inventory wholesale via Purchase Orders (POs). We often design a hybrid strategy where core volume lines leverage 1P while high-margin catalog lines utilize 3P Seller Central for agile pricing.

How do you protect and maintain Buy Box share? +

The Buy Box accounts for over 82% of all Amazon purchases. We implement strict MAP policy tracking, Brand Registry IP enforcement, counterfeit gating, rule-based algorithmic repricing, and FBA stock velocity controls to ensure your authorized listings maintain near-100% Buy Box share.

Which Amazon geographical marketplaces do you support? +

We actively manage Amazon operations across North America (Amazon.com, Amazon.ca), Europe (UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain), Australia (Amazon.com.au), and the Middle East (Amazon.ae in UAE, Amazon.sa in Saudi Arabia).
