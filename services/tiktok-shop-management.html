Frequently Asked Questions

TikTok Shop FAQs

How does TikTok Shop differ from traditional marketplaces like Amazon? +

Unlike intent-based marketplaces where shoppers search for a specific product, TikTok Shop generates demand organically through interest-based short video content, live broadcasts, and trusted creator recommendations. Consumers complete the checkout natively inside TikTok without leaving the app.

How do creator affiliate programs work on TikTok Shop? +

Through the TikTok Shop Affiliate Center, brands offer commission rates to qualified creators. Creators request free product samples, create authentic shoppable videos, and tag your product. When viewers purchase, TikTok automatically credits the creator's commission while your merchant account collects the net revenue.

How is order fulfillment handled on TikTok Shop? +

Brands can use Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) where TikTok manages warehousing and fast delivery, or fulfill orders via third-party logistics (3PL) and Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF). We integrate logistics systems to ensure strict dispatch SLA adherence.
