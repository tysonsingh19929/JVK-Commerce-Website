Frequently Asked Questions

Walmart Marketplace FAQs

Why should brands sell on Walmart Marketplace alongside Amazon? +

Walmart reaches over 120 million monthly unique digital shoppers in North America with significantly lower seller saturation than Amazon. With zero monthly recurring platform fees, it offers higher incremental margins and acts as a powerful revenue hedge against single-marketplace reliance.

How does Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) improve sales conversion? +

WFS stores, picks, packs, and ships orders with guaranteed nationwide 2-day delivery across the United States. Listings using WFS receive prominent 2-Day Delivery badging, preferential Buy Box positioning, and automated Walmart customer service, typically lifting sales velocity by 30% to 50%.

How do you achieve the Walmart Pro Seller Badge? +

The Pro Seller Badge requires maintaining an On-Time Delivery rate of 95%+, a seller-cancellation rate under 1.5%, competitive pricing, and consistent 90-day transaction volume. We actively manage catalog metrics and logistics to secure and maintain this high-converting trust mark.
