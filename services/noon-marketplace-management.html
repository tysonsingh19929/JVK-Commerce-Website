Noon Marketplace FAQs

Why is Noon critical for e-commerce growth in the Gulf region? +

Noon is the leading homegrown digital ecosystem in the Middle East, capturing immense market share across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt. Alongside Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa, having a first-class presence on Noon is essential for maximizing reach and capturing regional holiday peaks like Ramadan and Yellow Friday.

What is Fulfilled by Noon (FBN) and how does it compare to Cross-Dock? +

Fulfilled by Noon (FBN) stores your products inside Noon's regional fulfillment centers, granting the Noon Express badge, fast next-day delivery, and prioritized Buy Box weighting. Cross-Dock (FBN Direct) allows sellers to keep inventory in local warehouses and transfer items to Noon hubs only when orders occur. We configure the optimal model based on your SKU volume and storage profile.

What regulatory documentation is required to sell on Noon in UAE and KSA? +

Selling on Noon requires a valid commercial license (or international trading entity), registered VAT certificate from FTA (UAE) or ZATCA (Saudi Arabia), Saber platform conformity certificates for consumer goods into KSA, and brand authorization letters.
